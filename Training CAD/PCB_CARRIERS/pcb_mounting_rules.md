# PCB Mounting Rules

- Use actual PCB outline dimensions.
- Use actual mounting hole coordinates.
- Do not confuse electrical pads with mounting holes.
- Leave bottom clearance for solder joints and pins.
- Leave top clearance for components.
- Use reinforced standoffs.
- Keep board guides loose enough for assembly.
- Do not block connectors or solder pad access.
- Verify heat-set insert pilot diameter and depth with the actual insert before release.
- Confirm PCB fit with the real board and any soldered harness installed.
- Check that standoff insert depth leaves enough material and does not create assembly or heat damage risk.
