# Grommet Entry Lesson

## When To Use It

Use a circular grommet or cable gland entry when an enclosure needs a controlled wire pass-through.

## Common Mistakes

- Long rectangular wire openings.
- Multiple random wall gaps.
- No reinforcement around the entry.
- Hole diameter not matched to actual grommet.

## Key Parameters

- `hole_diameter`
- `wall_thickness`
- `boss_outer_diameter`
- `boss_thickness`
- `wall_width`
- `wall_height`

## Manufacturing Notes

Verify the grommet or cable gland datasheet. Add a boss/ring when the wall needs extra compression or stiffness.
