# Rounded Mount Ear Lesson

## When To Use It

Use rounded mounting ears when a housing or bracket must bolt to another structure while preserving a clean molded appearance.

## Common Mistakes

- Flat unsupported ears.
- Screw holes too close to the edge.
- Random truss fins.
- Sharp inside corners.
- Thin cantilever tabs.
- Forked or patched-on ear shapes that look crude even if they are thick.

## Key Parameters

- `ear_length`
- `ear_width`
- `ear_thickness`
- `hole_diameter`
- `boss_outer_diameter`
- `neck_width`
- `edge_rounding`
- `gusset_thickness`

## Manufacturing Notes

Use enough boss area for the screw head or washer. Tie the lug into the main body with a broad neck and clean gussets. A refined ear should read as one molded feature: oval/teardrop lug, raised boss, wide root, and two clear load-path gussets.
