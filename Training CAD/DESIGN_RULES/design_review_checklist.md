# Design Review Checklist

Before finalizing OpenSCAD or CAD geometry, check:

- Latest user feedback was followed.
- Rejected features were removed.
- Dimensions are based on facts.
- Assumptions are labeled.
- Load paths are real.
- Bosses are reinforced.
- Tabs are supported.
- Ribs and gussets are functional.
- Exterior openings are intentional.
- Wire strain relief is usable.
- No leak paths exist if water or dust matters.
- The part is printable.
- The part is serviceable.
- The part is compact but strong.
- Parameters are documented.
- Industrial-design refinements improve proportion, rim/corner treatment, and feature consistency without adding ornament.
- Style changes do not move fixed functional geometry.
- Actual fasteners, washers, grommets/cable glands, inserts, zip ties, and wire bundles are selected or clearly listed as blockers.
- Printed coupons or partial test sections are planned for fit-critical hardware.
- Vehicle-use designs include a vibration/shock validation plan before production release.
- The model still renders.
- Lessons or datasets were updated if reusable knowledge was produced.
