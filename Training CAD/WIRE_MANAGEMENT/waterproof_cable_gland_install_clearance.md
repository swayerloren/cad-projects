# Waterproof Cable Gland Install Clearance

When adding a waterproof cable gland to an enclosure, check both sides of the
wall.

Checklist:

- Thread hole diameter matches the selected gland.
- Outside flange can seat on a flat landing.
- Inside nut can sit flat.
- Tool/finger clearance exists around the nut.
- Internal rails, ribs, and floors do not block tightening.
- Wire bend radius is available after the gland.
- Seal/lid geometry is not damaged by the relief.

For compact enclosures, the inside nut often drives the packaging more than the
hole diameter.
