# Cable-Tie Saddles

Cable-tie saddles are clean low-profile strain-relief features.

Good saddle:

- Low base pad molded into the floor or wire lane.
- Raised bridge with side feet or rails around the tunnel.
- Real pass-under tunnel for the tie, with visible entry and exit.
- Enough clearance for the tie to pass through, loop over the wire bundle, and tighten.
- Accessible after the PCB is installed and wires are soldered.
- Clear side space between the saddle, the PCB edge, and the enclosure wall.
- Rounded edges.
- Optional small lead-in gussets.
- Clear routing that does not block the PCB.
- Translucent clearance preview when reviewing OpenSCAD geometry.

Bad saddle:

- Tiny slot.
- Tall tower.
- Exterior wall hole.
- Random block.
- Rail geometry where the zip tie cannot actually be threaded.
- Fake bridge shape with no physical pass-through path.
- Hidden saddle placed under or behind an installed PCB.
