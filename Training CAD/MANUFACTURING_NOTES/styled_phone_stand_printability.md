# Styled Phone Stand Printability

## Functional Areas

- front lip carries the phone's lower edge
- backrest carries the rear face
- side bolsters help center the phone
- cable cutout must stay clear of common charging connectors

## Print Risks

- very thin bases can flex
- decorative tube frames often need support
- harness holes and cutouts create local weak sections
- tall angled backs may need support depending on orientation

## Improvement Options

- thicken base or add underside ribs
- add rubber pad recesses
- make cable cutout size configurable
- keep bolster roots broad
- add optional support-free simplified rollcage
