# Glued Printed Gear Assemblies

## When It Works

Glued printed assemblies are acceptable for simple demonstration mechanisms when:

- loads are low
- gear replacement is not expected
- shafts are short
- alignment features are printed into the frame
- the user can test fit before applying glue

## Risks

- difficult repair after gear wear
- glue squeeze-out can foul rotating gears
- fixed alignment errors become permanent
- printed shafts wear faster than metal pins
- no easy way to tune backlash after assembly

## Better Serviceable Variant

Replace glued posts with:

- heat-set inserts
- screws or shoulder screws
- metal dowel pins
- printed or metal washers
- removable top plate

For OpenSCAD training examples, model both the simple glued version and an optional serviceable version.
