# Materials For Vehicle Use

For vehicle, UTV, outdoor, heat, or vibration environments:

- PLA is not preferred.
- PETG is better for general toughness.
- ASA is better for UV and outdoor exposure.
- ABS has better heat resistance but can be harder to print.
- Nylon can be strong and tough but needs dry filament and correct settings.
- TPU can be useful for seals, grommets, bumpers, and vibration isolation.
