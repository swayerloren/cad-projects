# Lid Relay Bracket Insert Bosses

When relays mount to the inside of a lid, use insert bosses that are designed for service and vibration.

Recommended pattern:

- two insert bosses per relay bracket
- boss OD around 7 mm for M3 inserts as a starting point
- insert hole and depth parameterized
- blind insert holes when possible so the top face remains sealed
- ribs connecting boss pairs to the lid panel
- enough relay-to-relay spacing for wiring and tool access

Service warning:

Relays mounted to a removable lid require harness slack, connectors, or a service loop. Do not let relay wiring pull directly on PCB solder joints when the lid is removed.
