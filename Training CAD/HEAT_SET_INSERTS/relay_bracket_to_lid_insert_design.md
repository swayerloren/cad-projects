# Relay Bracket To Lid Insert Design

When removable relay brackets fasten to a lid, keep the insert layout shared
between the lid and bracket files.

Checklist:

- define relay centers once
- derive bracket screw holes from those relay centers
- use M3 clearance holes in brackets
- use heat-set insert pilots in lid bosses
- keep brackets out of the lid production STL
- show bracket overlays only in preview mode

The bracket should be removable without disturbing the lid-to-base screws.
