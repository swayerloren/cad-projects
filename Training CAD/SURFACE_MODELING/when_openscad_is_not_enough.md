# When OpenSCAD Is Not Enough

OpenSCAD is strong for parametric mechanical geometry, but it is not always the right tool.

Use another workflow when the part depends on:

- controlled fillets and chamfers
- organic surfaces
- lofts and sweeps
- complex ergonomic shells
- sculpted product styling
- STEP-first handoff
- constrained sketch history
- assemblies with mating constraints

Use FreeCAD when the model can benefit from sketches, constraints, solid feature history, STEP export, and macro automation.

Use imported STL or mesh references when recreating complex organic parts, but keep the rebuilt CAD clean and functional.

Do not force complex surface styling into fragile OpenSCAD approximations unless the user specifically wants a code-only model.
