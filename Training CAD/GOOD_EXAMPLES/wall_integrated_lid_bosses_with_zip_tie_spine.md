# Good Example: Wall-Integrated Lid Bosses With Zip-Tie Spine

A better compact enclosure side zone aligns lid insert bosses with zip-tie clips on one wall-integrated system.

Good traits:

- insert bosses merge into the side wall or spine
- no separate row of standalone tall cylinders
- zip-tie clips remain serviceable
- side width is driven by shared service geometry, not stacked empty lanes
- lid and base use a shared fastener coordinate layout

COMMAND LINK example:

- fastener side inset moved from 10.0 mm to 8.0 mm
- side margin reduced from 15.40 mm to 14.25 mm
- body width reduced from 95.80 mm to 93.50 mm
