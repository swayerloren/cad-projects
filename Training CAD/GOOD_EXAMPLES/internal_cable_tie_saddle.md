# Internal Cable-Tie Saddle

## Summary

An internal cable-tie saddle uses a low floor pad, raised rails, and a real tunnel for the zip tie.

## Why It Matters

It provides wire strain relief without cutting through exterior walls or creating leak paths.

## Good Pattern

- Low-profile base molded into the floor or wire lane.
- Smooth bridge with side material around the tunnel.
- Usable pass-under tunnel clearance.
- Rounded/chamfered edges.
- Internal-only placement.
- Clear zip-tie path that can actually be threaded.

## Correct Design Rule

Wire strain relief should protect solder joints and should not compromise water/dust resistance.

## OpenSCAD/CAD Notes

Parameterize tunnel width and height around the actual zip tie size plus tolerance. A translucent clearance preview is useful during design review.

## Manufacturing Notes

Avoid tunnels too small for FDM printing. Add chamfers or radii to make tie insertion easier.
