# Good Example: Open-bottom Relay Top Retainer

Use this pattern when a relay or socket must be retained against a lid-mounted
insert pair without becoming permanently trapped.

Good traits:

- Two screw feet align to heat-set inserts.
- The center underside is open.
- A top bridge or strap holds the relay/socket down.
- Small capture lips can improve vibration retention.
- The retainer can be removed for service.

Validation checks:

- Clamp feet do not cross the O-ring seal keepout.
- Relay terminals and harness paths remain clear.
- Screw access is vertical and serviceable.
- Printed material is suitable for heat and vibration.
