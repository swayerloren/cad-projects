# Good Example: Simple Integrated Electronics Mounting Ear

A rugged electronics mounting ear should be simple and direct.

Good characteristics:

- wide rounded or teardrop tab
- one screw clearance hole
- low reinforced boss around the hole
- wide neck blended into the enclosure wall/floor
- simple side gussets that do not block screw access
- no decorative truss clutter
- no tall stacked boss rings

For vehicle/off-road modules, the mount should look like the load travels straight from the fastener into the enclosure body.
