# Circular Grommet Wire Entry

## Summary

A circular grommet or cable gland hole is a controlled wire-entry feature for an enclosure wall.

## Why It Matters

It is cleaner and more sealable than random wall gaps or long rectangular openings.

## Good Pattern

- One intentional circular opening.
- Sized to actual grommet or cable gland.
- Reinforced boss or ring.
- Placed where wire routing is serviceable.

## Correct Design Rule

Exterior wall openings should be deliberate and supported.

## OpenSCAD/CAD Notes

Parameterize hole diameter, boss diameter, boss thickness, and wall thickness.

## Manufacturing Notes

Verify grommet fit before final print. Add enough edge distance around the hole.
