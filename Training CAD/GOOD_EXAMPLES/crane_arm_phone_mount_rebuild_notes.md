# Good Example Notes: Crane Arm Phone Mount

Good practices captured:

- The rebuild did not raw-convert STL meshes.
- Each physical part maps to a named module.
- Assembly preview uses measured source centers.
- Individual overlays compare rebuilt modules against original STL envelopes.
- Adjustable phone and pivot parameters are exposed at the top.
- Limitations are documented where sculpted details were simplified.

This is a useful example for teaching the difference between dimensional reverse engineering and exact surface reproduction.
