# Serviceable Relay Retainer Brackets

Good relay retainers are separate serviceable parts, not permanent trapped
features.

Good pattern:

- heat-set inserts in the lid or mounting panel
- removable M3 screws
- retainer strap or clamp that can be removed per relay
- clearance for relay terminals and wiring
- parametric relay assumptions until hardware is measured
- separate production STL for brackets

Do not claim the bracket is final until the real relay/socket is measured and
vibration tested.
