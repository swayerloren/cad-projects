# Rounded Reinforced Mount Ear

## Summary

A clean rounded mounting ear uses a broad lug, raised screw boss, wide neck, and simple gussets.

## Why It Matters

The mount looks professional and carries fastener loads into the enclosure body without random fin geometry.

## Good Pattern

- Rounded rectangular, oval, or teardrop lug.
- Screw hole centered in a raised boss.
- Wide neck into the wall/floor.
- Two clean underside or side gussets when needed.
- Smooth transitions.

## Correct Design Rule

External mounts should be clean, supported, and connected to the main body through a visible load path.

## OpenSCAD/CAD Notes

Use rounded rectangles or hulls for molded-looking lugs. Keep screw holes parameterized. Avoid visual clutter: the ear should be one continuous lug and neck, not a rectangular tab with fins stuck onto it.

## Manufacturing Notes

Use washer area around the hole. Avoid sharp inside corners where the ear joins the enclosure.
