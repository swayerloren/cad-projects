# Grommet Hole With Inside And Outside Clearance

Good pattern:

- define the wall hole diameter
- define outside flange/gland-nut envelope
- define inside collar/flange envelope
- add a local inside relief when internal rails or ribs block the collar
- keep the relief from cutting through the exterior wall
- preserve sealing lands and structural features where possible
- preview the wire bend after the grommet

The clearance envelope should be larger than the rubber flange, not equal to it.
For example, a 24 mm collar with 2 mm radial margin needs a 28 mm clear zone.
