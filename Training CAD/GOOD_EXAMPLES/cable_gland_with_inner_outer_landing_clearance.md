# Good Example: Cable Gland With Inner / Outer Landing Clearance

A serviceable cable gland area provides:

- thread hole through the wall
- flat outside flange landing
- flat inside nut landing
- retaining nut clearance
- tool/finger clearance to tighten the nut
- wire bend clearance after entry

Good OpenSCAD practice:

- Use parameters for thread diameter, flange diameter, nut diameter, tool
  clearance, and relief depth.
- Use production cutouts to remove blocking rails/ribs.
- Use preview-only translucent hardware to validate fit.
- Document that physical test fitting is still required.
