# Good Example Notes: Racing Seat Phone Stand

Good practices:

- Treat aesthetic STL surfaces as reference, not as geometry to dump into SCAD.
- Preserve core function: phone support angle, front lip, cable cutout, and backrest.
- Parameterize both utility and style.
- Document where sculpted surfaces are simplified.
- Use overlay renders to compare silhouette and proportions.

This is a useful example for styled functional products where exact mesh reproduction is less important than editable design intent.
