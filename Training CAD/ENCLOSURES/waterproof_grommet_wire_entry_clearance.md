# Waterproof Grommet Wire Entry Clearance

## Rule

A circular wire-entry hole is not enough for a waterproof grommet or cable gland. Model and verify the surrounding hardware envelope:

- panel hole diameter
- wall thickness / grip range
- outside flange or gland nut diameter
- inside flange diameter
- tool/finger access outside
- wire bend radius inside
- conflict with sealing lands, screw bosses, PCB, and floor

## Practical Targets

For a nominal 16 mm panel hole:

- preview a 24 mm outside flange
- preview a 24 mm inside flange
- provide about 2 mm radial landing margin if possible
- chamfer the hole mouth so rubber is not cut by a sharp printed edge

Final dimensions must come from the actual grommet or cable gland datasheet and a printed fit test.
