# Waterproof Box Internal Carrier Rules

An internal carrier inside a waterproof box does not need to be the final sealed enclosure, but it should still be rugged and organized.

Rules:

- Do not create random wall gaps.
- Keep wire entry compatible with future grommets or cable glands.
- Keep exterior walls solid where possible.
- Use internal cable-tie saddles for strain relief.
- Keep mounting points outside the PCB area.
- Leave access for screws, inserts, wires, and service.
