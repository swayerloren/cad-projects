# Integrated Side-Wall Service Spine

Lesson from COMMAND LINK.

When an enclosure side wall needs wire retention and lid fasteners, do not create separate inboard lanes for every feature. A compact molded side-wall spine can combine:

- enclosure wall reinforcement
- zip-tie clips
- lid screw insert bosses
- local ribs and webs
- seal/rim clearance

The service spine should keep the exterior wall solid, keep zip-tie slots facing the inside, and keep lid insert holes accessible from above.

Check before accepting:

- PCB still fits.
- Wire lane remains usable.
- Zip ties can still be threaded and cut.
- Lid screw holes still align.
- The seal path remains intact.
- Production export excludes preview helpers.
