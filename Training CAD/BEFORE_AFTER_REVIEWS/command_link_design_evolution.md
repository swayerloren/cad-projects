# COMMAND LINK Design Evolution

## Summary

COMMAND LINK evolved from a simple PCB carrier into a rugged lower enclosure concept for an off-road UTV electronics module.

## Evolution

1. The project started with a flat PCB carrier.
2. Walls and a rugged lower enclosure were added.
3. Through-wall zip-tie slots were rejected.
4. A long rectangular wire opening was rejected.
5. One circular short-side grommet hole was required.
6. Ugly/blocky zip-tie mounts were rejected.
7. Internal cable-tie saddle style was required.
8. Ugly external mount pods were rejected.
9. Clean rounded reinforced automotive-style mount ears were required.

## Lessons

- Strength needs load paths, not random bulk.
- Wire strain relief must protect solder joints and avoid leak paths.
- Exterior openings should be deliberate.
- Mounts need bosses, necks, gussets, and smooth transitions.
- Good geometry should look molded and intentional.
