# Reinforced Mount Pods

Reinforced mount pods can be useful, but they must not become ugly random fin geometry.

Good pods:

- Have a screw boss.
- Tie into the wall and floor.
- Use clear ribs or gussets.
- Preserve a clean molded appearance.

Avoid:

- Fork shapes.
- Jagged fins.
- Decorative trusses.
- Oversized bulk with no service benefit.
