# Articulated Phone Mount Design Notes

## Load Path

Phone load moves from:

1. phone bottom shelf
2. backing plate
3. gripper or frame
4. pivot holes and link arms
5. base or accessory mount

The highest-risk areas are the pivot holes and shelf roots.

## Design Rules

- Put extra material around pivot holes.
- Avoid thin links below 3 mm unless loads are low.
- Add washer pads or bosses around screw holes.
- Keep phone contact faces broad and smooth.
- Add clearance parameters for screws, printed pins, and phone thickness.
- Use mirrored modules for left/right arms where possible.

## Print Notes

Thin arms usually print best flat, with high perimeter counts around holes. Phone shelves need enough wall thickness at the lip so phone weight does not split layer lines.
