# Unsupported Zip-Tie Towers

## Summary

Tall standalone zip-tie towers are fragile and often look like hobby geometry.

## Why It Matters

They can snap under tie tension or vibration and can block PCB installation.

## Bad Pattern

- Vertical tower.
- No base support.
- No integration with wall/floor.
- Slot too small or awkward.

## Correct Design Rule

Use low molded saddle mounts or supported internal anchors.

## OpenSCAD/CAD Notes

Keep the tie feature low, broad, and serviceable.

## Manufacturing Notes

Avoid tall thin features loaded across layer lines.
