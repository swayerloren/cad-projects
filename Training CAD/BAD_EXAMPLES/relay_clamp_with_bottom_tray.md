# Bad Example: Relay Clamp With Bottom Tray

A removable relay clamp for a lid-mounted relay should not include a floor or
tray under the relay unless the relay is intentionally being placed into a
separate carrier.

Failure pattern:

- Screw feet are connected by a bottom floor.
- The relay sits in or over a tray instead of being retained by a top bridge.
- Terminals and service access can be blocked.
- The clamp becomes more like a cage than a simple removable retainer.

Corrective rule:

- Use two screw feet and an open-bottom top retainer bridge.
- Add only small capture lips or pads where needed.
- Let the relay/socket sit against the lid or designated relay pad.
