# Bad Example: Separate Inboard Lid Post Lane

Problem:

The side wall has zip-tie clips, but lid screw insert posts are placed farther inboard as a separate row of tall cylinders. This wastes width and makes the model look less like a molded electronics enclosure.

Symptoms:

- visible empty channel between wall features and lid posts
- tall standalone insert columns
- wire lane, zip-tie lane, and screw lane are all separate
- enclosure becomes wider without adding useful service clearance

Fix:

Move lid insert bosses into the wall service spine and update the lid fastener positions through shared dimensions.
