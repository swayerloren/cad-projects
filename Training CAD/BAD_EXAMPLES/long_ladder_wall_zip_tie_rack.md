# Bad Example: Long Ladder Wall Zip-Tie Rack

Failure pattern:

- Many repeated wall slots are placed close together.
- The feature reads as a vent, rack, grille, or ladder.
- It looks bulky and visually busy.
- It is harder to inspect and harder to thread cleanly.
- It does not match a molded individual wall clip.

Why it fails:

Wire retention should look like a compact service feature, not a full wall panel. Six-per-side layouts can be useful only when the harness truly needs dense tie points, but they should not be the default if the product target is a clean wall-integrated clip.

Corrective rule:

Use individual wall-integrated zip-tie clips with clear spacing, rounded upper/lower slots, and a solid center bridge.
