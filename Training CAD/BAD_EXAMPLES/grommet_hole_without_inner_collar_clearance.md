# Grommet Hole Without Inner Collar Clearance

Bad pattern:

- model only the circular wall hole
- preview only the outside flange
- leave internal ribs, rails, floors, or ledges directly behind the hole
- assume a grommet will fit because the hole diameter is correct

A waterproof grommet or cable gland needs both sides checked. The inside collar
or gland nut can be blocked by internal geometry even when the through-hole is
the correct diameter.
