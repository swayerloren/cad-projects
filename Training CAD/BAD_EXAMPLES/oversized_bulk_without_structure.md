# Oversized Bulk Without Structure

## Summary

Making everything larger or thicker does not automatically create a better part.

## Why It Matters

Oversized bulk can still flex or crack if load paths are poor.

## Bad Pattern

- Huge solid walls.
- Thick tabs with no neck or gusset.
- Massive bosses with no support.
- Lost service clearance.

## Correct Design Rule

Improve load paths, ribs, gussets, boss support, transitions, and print orientation first.

## OpenSCAD/CAD Notes

Use parameters to tune strength deliberately, not randomly.

## Manufacturing Notes

Large solid sections print slowly and can warp. Injection molded parts may sink.
