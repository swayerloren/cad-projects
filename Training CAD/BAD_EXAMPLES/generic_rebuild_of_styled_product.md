# Generic Rebuild Of Styled Product

## Bad Pattern

A styled STL rebuild fails when it only matches bounding boxes or rough volumes.

Common symptoms:

- product silhouette is generic
- key openings are missing
- color/part identity is ignored
- functional placeholder is not tested
- base or support structure is flattened into a rectangle
- tubes or roll cages become random straight lines
- the result exports but does not look like the reference product

## Racing Seat Phone Stand Failure

The racing seat phone stand cannot be rebuilt as a generic chair or cradle. Its identity depends on:

- racing bucket seat silhouette
- harness/pass-through openings
- red/black seat color separation
- front phone catch
- gray side rails
- screw/bolt holes and boss pads
- triangular rail truss webbing
- black tube roll cage with diagonal braces

## Rule

For styled products, visual accuracy review must check silhouette, openings, color/part separation, and function. Bounding-box match alone is not acceptable.
