# Bad Example: Overcomplicated External Mount Ears

Avoid external mounting ears that look like stacked pads, tall boss rings, skinny arms, and decorative truss clutter.

Why this fails:

- The load path from screw to enclosure wall is visually and mechanically unclear.
- Skinny necks become vibration weak points.
- Tall stacked bosses add stress risers and print time.
- Decorative ribs can block screw/washer access.
- Complex intersections are harder to print and harder to tool later.

Better approach: use a wide integrated tab, one screw hole, one low boss, and simple ribs that feed load directly into the wall/floor.
