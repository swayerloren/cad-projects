# Weak Flat Mount Tabs

## Summary

A flat tab with a screw hole sticking out from a wall is usually weak in vibration or shock.

## Why It Matters

The tab can flex, crack at the wall, split around the screw, or fail along print layers.

## Bad Pattern

- Thin unsupported ear.
- Screw hole near the edge.
- No boss.
- No broad neck.
- No gusset or load path.

## Correct Design Rule

Use a rounded reinforced mounting ear with a boss, wide neck, and clean gussets.

## OpenSCAD/CAD Notes

Do not just extrude a rectangle and subtract a hole.

## Manufacturing Notes

Layer orientation and screw clamp force can make this failure worse.
