# Through-Wall Zip-Tie Holes

## Summary

Zip-tie slots cut through the exterior wall create leak paths and weaken the wall.

## Why It Matters

Dust and water resistance are compromised, and the exterior wall loses continuity.

## Bad Pattern

- Slots through the enclosure wall.
- Exterior visible tie holes.
- Tie path doubles as leak path.

## Correct Design Rule

Use internal-only cable-tie saddles or molded internal anchors.

## OpenSCAD/CAD Notes

Subtractive slots should cut only internal anchor geometry, not the exterior shell.

## Manufacturing Notes

Use enough tunnel clearance for real zip ties and print tolerance.
