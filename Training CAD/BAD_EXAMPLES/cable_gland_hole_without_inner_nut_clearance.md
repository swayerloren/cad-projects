# Bad Example: Cable Gland Hole Without Inner Nut Clearance

A waterproof cable gland needs more than a circular wall hole.

Failure pattern:

- The thread hole is modeled.
- The outside collar/flange is modeled.
- The inside retaining nut is ignored.
- Internal rails, floors, ribs, or wire guides block the nut.
- There is no room to rotate or tighten the nut.

Corrective rule:

- Model the threaded body, outside flange, inside retaining nut, tool/finger
  clearance, and wire bend path.
- Cut a real production relief for internal obstructions.
- Keep preview hardware out of production exports.
