# Errors And Fixes

## Phone bottom depth

Initial rebuild made the phone bottom shelf deeper in Y because the adjustable phone-thickness lip extended past the measured STL envelope.

Fix: kept the lip inside the measured 13 mm Y envelope and re-exported the part.

## Simplified gripper jaws

The gripper jaws include sculpted serrations and organic side profiles in the STL.

Fix: modeled a simplified clamp feature with pivot holes and documented the remaining shape mismatch.
