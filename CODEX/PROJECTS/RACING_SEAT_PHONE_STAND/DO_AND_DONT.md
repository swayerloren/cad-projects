# Do And Do Not

## Do

- Verify exact source and export paths in `PATHS.md`.
- Preserve original STL references and photos.
- Check visual accuracy against references before claiming the style is correct.

## Do Not

- Move reference STLs/photos without an approved migration.
- Delete earlier rebuild versions.
- Force organic/styled surfaces into OpenSCAD if FreeCAD or another workflow is more appropriate.
